## Context

When an architecture decision needs to be made but the answer is unclear or potentially controversial or expensive or risky

## Problem

Collective ownerships solves lack of accountability. Collective ownership is defined below as the self-selecting group that comes together to make a decision.

## Solution

Self-selecting group proposes a solution with problem context, options, trade-offs, the selected option based on real explorations. Take feedback, document feedback. Accept & modify, reject & move forward with justification.
