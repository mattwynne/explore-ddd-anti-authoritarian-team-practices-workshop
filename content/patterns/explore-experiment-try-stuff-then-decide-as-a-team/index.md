## Context

In software development this is useful most of the time.

## Problem

This deals with "analysis paralysis". We learn by including "doing" in our analysis. This is a way to discover, rather than just the thinking.

## Solution

Everyone who has an idea to try snaps it. We try all the ideas before we decide which one becomes really (obviously fantastic). With an even better idea once normally we will come up early ideas. The ideas grow. We have tried our early ideas.
