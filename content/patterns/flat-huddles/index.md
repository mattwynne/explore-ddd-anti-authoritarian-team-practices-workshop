## Context

When making big picture decisions impacting the whole group or team

## Problem

Suggestion for improvements from the bottom without being dictated from top down management. May remove implicit stressor, fear of manager.

## Solution

Manager or leader fields questions in a humble manner. Recognizes knowledge and experience of his/her team. Thanks team, allows team to debate. Can explain why decisions were made. Practice with small things.
