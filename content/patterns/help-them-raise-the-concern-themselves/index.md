## Context

Design review meeting (group setting) You have experience to notice a problem with the design.

## Problem

You want to bring up the problem without making anyone feel unsafe.

## Solution

Find a way to communicate the problem to the design owner before the meeting to give them an opportunity to bring up the problem themselves. The sooner you can give the feedback, the better. (If they ignore the problem, you can fall back to bringing it up in the group.)
