## Context

A facilitator will lead an optional meeting. There is no consequence for missing the meeting.

## Problem

For the save of order or efficiency, the facilitator wants to set the format or rules of the meeting - but wants to do so without coersion.

## Solution

Make the rules and format clear before the meeting. Participants opt-in to the rules (and the facilitator's authority) by attending.
