## Context

Allowing someone to suggest changing an established structure/rule, but follow the existing rule to change it.

## Problem

The group may not want to change the rule and make the person propose have no say.

## Solution

Anyone can propose a change to the established rule and explain why they think it is a good change. Everyone has a chance to give their feedback on it.
