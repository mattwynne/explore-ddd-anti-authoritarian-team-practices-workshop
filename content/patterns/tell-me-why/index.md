## Context

Want a team (all members) to feel empowered to use their good judgment in all situations.

## Problem

People don't understand the relationship between what they do and the mission.

## Solution

Leaders help people be successful, not doing the work, but explaining why the work needs to be done. Show the connections between outcomes and goals.
