## Context

Team is misaligned with how an org is successful or stakeholders are served

## Problem

Misalignment, frustrations when decisions are "good" but only w/ context about the ecosystem.

## Solution

- Simple statement describing how value or benefit or sustainability is achieved. ex: VA care for Veteran's and their families.- Allow people to self select (if they can) - Maximize Shareholder Value- No generic mission statements, doesn't work for every org.- North Star Metric if possible
