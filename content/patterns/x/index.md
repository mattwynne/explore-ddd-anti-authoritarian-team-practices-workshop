## Context

Often on a project or team, the most Sr. person decides what to do how to do it & so on. Splitting seniority w/ the person w/ project context can push a team to not just by default follow one person on the team.

## Problem

Anchors Stay on a project as long as the engagement runs & "anchor" the project. They have enduring context, but don't really "lead" the team. They might be Sr. but might not. Roles exist on a project, but the Anchor isn't the leader necessarily.

## Solution

As people move on/off a project as scope changes, the project Anchor helps shape decisions w/o necessarily being the most "qualified". Anchors don't have a leadership role, but are there to help share context & ensure the team is productive & empowered.
